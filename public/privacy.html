DA20 Planner: privacy

DA20 Planner is a non-commercial training aid for weight & balance and take-off / landing performance of the Diamond DA20-C1.

Without an account

Everything you enter is kept only in your own browser on your own device. Nothing is sent to us.

With an account

If you create an account (with email and password, or with Google), we store:

  • your email address and name, to sign you in;
  • the load sheets you choose to save: aircraft, weights, fuel, aerodrome conditions and the calculated results.

Signing in with Google only shares your name, email address and profile picture with us. We don't access anything else in your Google account.

Your saved load sheets can only be read by your own account. Data is stored with Supabase (database and sign-in) and is not sold or shared with anyone else.

Weather

METAR / TAF requests send only the aerodrome codes you enter to a relay that fetches public weather from aviationweather.gov. Text you paste from a NAIPS briefing stays in your browser.

Deleting your data

You can delete any saved load sheet in the app. To delete your account and all its data, contact ericye223378@gmail.com.
